Content Partnership Proposal

T'Dane Gordon x Best E. Villas

A content collaboration built around a stay at Best E. Villas, with T'Dane's own videographer shooting and editing the full package. Produced content, not just creator footage, at no extra sourcing cost to you.

Overview

Who this is with

T'Dane Gordon is a Jamaican content creator and Miss Jamaica World alumna, with an active following built on lifestyle, travel, and personal content across Instagram and TikTok.

T'Dane will be accompanied by her own dedicated videographer for the full stay, so the content is professionally shot and edited rather than relying on creator-only footage, with no need for Best E. Villas to separately source or hire a local videographer.

The Fit

Why Best E. Villas

Best E. Villas is positioned as comfortable, affordable, and family-run, with self-contained units across two coasts: Prospect and St. Silas Heights on the West Coast, and Providence on the South Coast. That positioning is a strength for this content package, it means the content can be value-based rather than forced into a luxury-resort frame the brand isn't focused on selling.

The two-coast spread is also a built-in content hook most competitors don't have, a natural West vs. South comparison across a single stay.
